# Icon sources

- `icons/diamond-pickaxe.png` is the Minecraft 1.21.4 item texture mirrored by
  [PrismarineJS/minecraft-assets](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-assets).
- The five textures in `activity/` are Minecraft 1.21.4 block textures from
  the same mirror. They represent stone, iron ore, gold ore, diamond block,
  and emerald block in increasing contribution levels.
- The source SVGs and rendered PNGs in `languages/` come from
  [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), which is distributed under
  the MIT license. Individual logos remain trademarks of their owners.

The profile generator embeds the PNG files into its generated SVG. It does not
redraw these icons with Python.
